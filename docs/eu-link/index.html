What India sends to Europe

India is one of the three largest sources of CBAM goods entering the EU. Nine in ten euros of it are steel. Volumes were already falling in 2025, before the first certificate is bought.

Where CBAM and India's CCTS meet

Each band is one sector. Follow it to see whether it faces the EU border charge, India's own targets, or both. Plants facing both can use one verified emissions dataset for both, and the CBAM rules provide for the carbon price paid at home to be deducted.

What would the CBAM bill be?

CBAM charges the emissions in each tonne, minus a free allowance that EU producers also get. That allowance shrinks every year and is gone by 2034, so the bill climbs even if nothing else changes. Pick a product and move the sliders.

Default values: Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2621 (corrected by 2026/1740), with the mark-up of 10%, 20% and 30% in 2026, 2027 and 2028 onward (1% for fertilisers). The global market for Paris Agreement credits

Under Article 6, countries trade emission reductions with each other. Buyers such as Japan, Singapore and Switzerland have signed deals with dozens of host countries. India has two so far, which leaves room to grow.

Where India stands
  • Japan (Joint Crediting Mechanism): agreed in the India–Japan Joint Vision of August 2025; operating rules adopted in June 2026. India's first framework able to produce credits for transfer abroad.
  • Second agreement: the UNEP pipeline lists two Indian agreements; an India–South Korea deal was reported in April 2026.
  • Article 6.4: no Indian activity is yet among the 416 approved, but 458 Indian CDM projects have asked to move across (next section).

The EU carbon removal standard (CRCF)

Since 7 May 2026 the EU certifies permanent carbon removals (biochar, direct air capture with storage, bio-energy with carbon capture and storage) under one public method. Its location rules point to EU-based projects, so Indian projects cannot use it directly today, but it is the yardstick European buyers will compare Indian biochar and removal credits against.
