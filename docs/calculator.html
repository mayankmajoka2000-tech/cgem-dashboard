Market assumptions

Price(t) = Price(2026) × [(1 + demand growth) ÷ (1 + supply growth)]^(years ÷ elasticity) × (1 + shock from 2028). The band widens with the square root of years ahead. The growth rule is the one in the workbook’s Supply and demand sheet. A scenario, not a forecast.
