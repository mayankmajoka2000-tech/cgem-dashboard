Help set India’s carbon price

Most Indian carbon credit deals are private, so every public benchmark guesses. Share your deal prices with us in confidence and the open C-GEM price becomes more accurate for everyone, including you.

How to contribute

  1. Ask for accessOpen the submission form below and request access, or ask the C-GEM team to invite you. We add each contributor by email.
  2. Submit your pricesOne entry per deal: credit type, price, month and a few optional details. It takes about two minutes.
  3. Get the benefitEarly access to each model release and a confidential valuation of your own projects or purchases.

Our promise

  • Only the C-GEM team reads submissions. Other contributors cannot see yours.
  • We never publish a project name, a counterparty or a single deal. A price appears only inside an aggregate of at least three contributors.
  • We sign an NDA on request, and delete any submission you ask us to remove.
  • Each price is checked against the model before it is used, and the effect on accuracy is published in the version history.
