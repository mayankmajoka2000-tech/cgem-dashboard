The price equation

Robust hedonic model on log price: 0.8 × Huber regression + 0.2 × hierarchical ridge with literature priors, trained on 443 public prices (2020–2026, each older year weighted 0.7×).

ln(price) = 2.000 + 0.001 (2026) − 0.274 (Asia) + 0.152 (India) + type effect − 0.171 (recent vintage) + 0.351 if removal credit + old-vintage adjustment if vintage is more than 5 years old + high-rating effect if rated high, or −0.350 if rated low + 0.574 if Article 6 authorised / CORSIA price = exp(ln price) × 2.255 if ICVCM CCP-approved P10 = price × exp(−1.049) P90 = price × exp(type p90_log)

Every coefficient is in price_model.json; the Python version is cgem_price_model.py.

Definitions and limits
Expected price
Wholesale price of the credit at 2026 market level from the C-GEM model. The register applies the credit type’s equation with a recent vintage and no rating; use the calculator for a project-specific price.
P10 and P90
One price in ten falls below P10 and one in ten above P90. Indian ranges are calibrated on Indian deal prices (82% inside the range when tested).
Integrity score
C-GEM screening score from public registry data: type risk, CCP status, delivery record, vintage, co-benefit label, activity and Article 6 match. Not a rating.
Demand score
Expected share of a project’s credits that get retired, from the C-GEM demand model.
Sales value
Expected price × risk-adjusted annual credits × demand score.
Left out
Private deals and paywalled assessment series; CCTS carbon credit certificates, which have no traded price yet.
